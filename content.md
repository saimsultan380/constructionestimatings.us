What Is an Xactimate Estimate? Line Items, Price Lists, and Overhead and Profit Explained
An Xactimate estimate is an itemized repair or rebuild estimate built from line items, quantities, and a regional price list. It lists each task, material, and unit cost, plus tax and overhead and profit, so carriers, contractors, and property owners can see how the total was reached.
Verisk publishes monthly pricing data for more than 460 geographic regions. That is why the right price list for your location and month matters as much as the line items themselves.

Line items
Each task, material, or service, such as drywall replacement or shingle removal
Quantity and unit
Measured amounts in SF, LF, SQ, or EA
Category and selector codes
The exact item and specification chosen
Tax
Sales tax applied to materials, where it applies
Overhead and profit
The general contractor’s overhead and profit, when it is part of the scope
